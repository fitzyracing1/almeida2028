About Joshua

Joshua Almeida is an independent developer and small-business owner from New Bedford, Massachusetts. He builds software, runs a small business, and believes government should work as openly and reliably as good code does.

Priorities

The ideas driving this campaign:

Open-source government

  • Publicly funded code open by default
  • Open APIs for federal data
  • Fund maintainers of critical open infrastructure

Small business and indie builders

  • Simpler taxes for sole proprietors
  • Portable health coverage for the self-employed
  • Fair access to federal contracts

AI and developer tools

  • Transparent, interoperable AI standards
  • Protect developer and researcher freedom
  • Digital skills in every community

Coastal and gateway-city revival

  • Fishing, offshore wind, and ports
  • Broadband and tech jobs in smaller cities
  • Tech and trade ties with Portugal and the Lusophone diaspora
